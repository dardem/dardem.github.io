CV of Failures

You might not believe it (well, of course you can), but I fail. We all fail.

The problem with modern IT, academic, and AI culture is that we have forgotten how to let people simply make mistakes and learn from them. We have also slipped into a culture where we believe only in the straight path. Yet none of the great people’s paths were straightforward. Neither is mine, and neither is yours. And that is okay.

It is not about avoiding failure — it is about overcoming it. As the saying goes, you will never get up if you never fall ;)

Here is my list of fuckups and what I learnt from them. Maybe you will learn from my failures too.

Jun 2026
Paper

Brutal desk rejection of the PluraMath paper

Desk-rejected at ARR — just because the appendix was in a one-column format.

What I learntEven if you submit to ARR cycles constantly, cross-check in every possible way (even with LLMs) that your paper satisfies all the requirements. However, nowadays ARR is becoming overall unreasonable.
Oct 2025
Job

Not selected for an assistant professorship at TU Wien

After the in-person interview, I was not selected for the position.

What I learntThere were many reasons, but next time will try harder to find common ground between myself and the place I am applying to.
Sep 2025
Grant

Did not get into the Emmy Noether AI programme

I did not dedicate enough time to the proposal; it was too general and not focused on AI (NLP is not AI and AI is not just NLP ;)).

What I learntSpent enough time on a proposal, you can use LLMs, but in reasonable way (ie, for search), read carefully the grants calls and understand what it means.
