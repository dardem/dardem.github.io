CV of Failures

You might not believe it (well, of course you can), but I fail. We all fail.

The problem with modern IT, academic, and AI culture is that we have forgotten how to let people simply make mistakes and learn from them. We have also slipped into a culture where we believe only in the straight path. Yet none of the great people’s paths were straightforward. Neither is mine, and neither is yours. And that is okay.

It is not about avoiding failure — it is about overcoming it. As the saying goes, you will never get up if you never fall ;)
