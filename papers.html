Data-Efficient Hate Speech Detection via Cross-Lingual Nearest Neighbor Retrieval with Limited Labeled Data

Faeze Ghorbanpour, Daryna Dementieva, Alexander Fraser. Proceedings of the 2025 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing

This paper tackles hate speech detection in low-resource languages by combining cross-lingual transfer learning with nearest-neighbor retrieval. Starting from a small set of labeled examples in the target language, the method retrieves the most relevant examples from a large multilingual pool to augment training data. Tested across eight languages, the approach consistently outperforms models trained on target-language data alone, often beats the current state-of-the-art, and remains highly efficient — sometimes needing as few as 200 retrieved instances — while being easy to scale and adapt to new languages.
