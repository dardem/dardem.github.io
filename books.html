Books

I read a lot — news, blogs, books.

When people ask me which book is my favourite, of course I can’t name just one. What I can give you is a list of books that deeply influenced me during my studies, opened new perspectives on the world, or were simply fun and fascinating to read.

And don’t worry if you are not reading much right now. I have had years when I read a lot (up to 100 books) and years when I read only a few. It comes and goes with the different periods of life. Still, some of these books helped me find meaning in dark times, and some truly empowered me for life. I hope they will be useful for you as well!

Man's Search for Meaning

Viktor E. Frankl

An ode to the fate of an immigrant fleeing war. It helps you understand that your problems of fleeing war are not isolated; people have faced similar situations before..

The Night in Lisbon

Erich Maria Remarque

Really helped me to understand how people can overcome catastrophic events like war and still find meaning in life.

Surely You’re Joking, Mr. Feynman!

Richard P. Feynman

A must-read for everyone who want to do science, especially, PhD. A light book about life, sciece, and life with science. Wisdom and love to life from incedible person.

Siddhartha

Hermann Hesse

In my humble opinion, very useful, especially, for young people, to understand life. Like, all of it.
