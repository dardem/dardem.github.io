Books

I read a lot — news, blogs, books.

When people ask me which book is my favourite, of course I can’t name just one. What I can give you is a list of books that deeply influenced me during my studies, opened new perspectives on the world, or were simply fun and fascinating to read.

And don’t worry if you are not reading much right now. I have had years when I read a lot (up to 100 books) and years when I read only a few. It comes and goes with the different periods of life. Still, some of these books helped me find meaning in dark times, and some truly empowered me for life. I hope they will be useful for you as well!

It's a Rich Man's World

Female Invest Team

Another book from Female Invest. Why money are still broken for women and how to fix it. An absolute recommend for, actually, everyone.

The Book You Want Everyone You Love to Read

Philippa Perry

I was just looking for something to read in summer and just randomly discovered this book on a bookshelf. Yes, you and everyone you love do want to read this book: an easy read with stories how to approach various situations in life and a lot of nice advices for relationships.

Atomic Habits

James Clear

Yes, it is a self-help book and it is... helpful. Many advice of these book became famous posts on social media. I would recommend to read the whole book: many points mentioned are applicable for a general life, however, also can be incredibly helpful on how to organise yourself and stay connected to life during your PhD.

Man's Search for Meaning

Viktor E. Frankl

An ode to the fate of an immigrant fleeing war. It helps you understand that your problems of fleeing war are not isolated; people have faced similar situations before.

Girls Just Wanna Have Funds: A Feminist's Guide to Investing

Female Invest Team

In a clear language, how money and funds work, with special advice how to fix it for women. Easy and super pleasant read, a fresh and modern view on money. An absolute recommend for all women.

The Night in Lisbon

Erich Maria Remarque

Really helped me to understand how people can overcome catastrophic events like war and still find meaning in life.

The Vegetarian

Han Kang

It is about feminism. Got Han Kang a Nobel Price. Yes, you should read it.

Surely You’re Joking, Mr. Feynman!

Richard P. Feynman

A must-read for everyone who want to do science, especially, PhD. A light book about life, sciece, and life with science. Wisdom and love to life from an incedible person.

Siddhartha

Hermann Hesse

In my humble opinion, very useful, especially, for young people, to understand life. Like, all of it.
